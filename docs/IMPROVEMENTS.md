# Ways to make Thambi better

Brainstormed 17 November 2026. 110 ideas in ten groups. **Bold** marks the ones worth doing first. "Built" notes what already exists so nothing is done twice.

## A. Learning that works (the reason to choose Thambi)

1. **Exam planner page**: past papers in, repeated questions and a day-by-day plan out. The logic is built and tested; it needs its screen.
2. **Teach-back mode**: you explain a topic, Thambi finds the gaps and asks two follow-up questions.
3. **Daily review reminder** at a time you choose, showing how many cards are due.
4. Quiz difficulty that adapts: more "why" and "what if" once recall questions are easy.
5. **Weak-topic map**: which units you get wrong most, from quiz and card history, all computed locally.
6. Multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank quiz types, marked on the Mac for free.
7. Diagram questions: label the parts of the built-in 3D heart, cell or engine.
8. Formula sheet per subject, pulled from your materials with what each symbol means.
9. "Explain like I'm new" and "explain like the examiner" as two buttons on any answer.
10. Worked-example mode for numerical problems: one step at a time, you try the next step first.
11. Mnemonic maker for lists you keep forgetting.
12. Mock exam: timed paper built from your past papers' pattern, marked by section.
13. Model answers for past questions in 2, 5, 10 or 16-mark shape (the marks format is built).
14. Mistake notebook: every wrong answer with the correct idea, reviewable before the exam.
15. Interleaving: a daily mixed quiz across subjects, which research shows beats studying one at a time.
16. Pre-test before reading a unit, so you know what to look for.
17. Confidence rating on quiz answers, to catch "sure but wrong".
18. Unit summary at three lengths: one line, one paragraph, one page.
19. Glossary per subject with search.
20. Reading tracker: which pages of each PDF you have asked about or been quizzed on.

## B. Library and notes

21. **⌘K search** across every page, note and card.
22. **Lecture to notes**: record a class, transcribe on the Mac, get clean notes with timestamps.
23. **Import from iPhone** with Continuity Camera: snap a page, it lands in the subject.
24. Rename a subject and drag a material onto a subject.
25. Dismiss all failed imports at once; retry one.
26. Web page and YouTube-transcript import by link (the page text only, fetched on request).
27. Highlight a passage in the PDF view and turn it into a card or a question.
28. Split view: PDF on the left, Ask on the right.
29. Note templates: Cornell notes, lab report, case brief.
30. Backlinks between notes and the pages they came from.
31. Handwriting quality warning when a photo is too blurred to read reliably.
32. Tamil and Hindi text recognition for scanned regional-language notes.
33. Duplicate detection when the same file is added twice under another name.
34. Export notes to PDF and Word; export cards to Anki (the Anki format is built).
35. Print-ready revision sheets, two columns.
36. Folder watch: a "Thambi" folder that imports anything dropped into it.
37. Syllabus import: paste the syllabus, get units that materials and plans attach to.
38. Storage screen: how much space each subject uses, with one-click clean-up.

## C. Asking and answers

39. **Answer language setting in Ask** (Tamil, Hindi, Tanglish and others; the setting exists, the wiring does not).
40. **Offline answers** from a model on the Mac, when one is already installed.
41. Follow-up chips under an answer: "Give an example", "Shorter", "Quiz me on this".
42. Ask about a selection: select text anywhere, press a shortcut, ask.
43. Saved conversations per subject, searchable (kept on the Mac).
44. Pin an answer to the top of a subject.
45. Compare two sources when your notes and textbook disagree.
46. "Where is this in my book?": a search that only returns pages, costing nothing.
47. Show which passages were sent with each question, for full transparency.
48. A daily spend limit you set, with a clear stop when reached.
49. Equation rendering for LaTeX in answers.
50. Code answers with a copy button and language label.
51. Read an answer aloud with the local voice.
52. Concept map of a subject, drawn by the existing drawing engine.
53. Spoken revision: listen to a unit as a ten-minute walk-through.
54. Citation confidence: flag an answer sentence that no source supports.

## D. Friends and studying together

55. **Verification code when adding a friend**, so both see the same four words and know nobody is in between.
56. **Study rooms**: up to eight friends in one focus session with a shared timer.
57. Share a quiz: send the questions, compare scores after.
58. Explain-it-to-me requests: a friend asks, you answer with a note or a model, they mark it helpful.
59. Shared deck that stays in sync between friends.
60. Class groups: one subject, many friends, shared past papers.
61. Weekly friendly leaderboard by focus minutes, opt-in, friends only.
62. Presence that respects you: "Do not disturb while focusing" holds messages until the timer ends.
63. Reactions and replies in chat; search in a conversation.
64. Send a photo of a handwritten working.
65. Annotate a shared 3D model: point at a part and leave a label for your friend.
66. Block and report, ready for when friends work over the internet.
67. Internet friends through an end-to-end encrypted relay (see PUBLIC-RELEASE.md).
68. Invite link or QR code to add a friend who is not nearby.
69. Study streak with a friend: both studied today.
70. Export or delete a whole conversation.

## E. Motivation and habits

71. **Today page**: cards due, today's plan tasks, one suggested quiz, friends studying now.
72. Weekly report: minutes, cards, quiz accuracy, strongest and weakest unit.
73. Streak freeze: one rest day a week that does not break the streak.
74. Goals: minutes per day or cards per day, with a quiet ring in the sidebar.
75. Break timer after a focus session, and a long break after four.
76. Menu bar countdown while a focus session runs.
77. Focus mode that hides everything except the current task.
78. Exam countdown in the sidebar.
79. Calendar export of the study plan (.ics file).
80. Gentle catch-up: when you miss a plan day, the plan redistributes instead of scolding.

## F. Design and usability

81. **Guided first run**: add one file, ask one question, take one quiz, in under two minutes.
82. **Empty states that teach**, with a sample subject you can try and delete.
83. Keyboard shortcuts for everything, with a cheat sheet on ⌘/.
84. Command palette for actions as well as search.
85. Toolbar with back and forward between pages.
86. Resizable sidebar and remembered window layout.
87. Text size setting for answers and notes.
88. Full VoiceOver pass and visible keyboard focus everywhere.
89. Reduce-motion and increase-contrast support in the study window.
90. Undo for delete, everywhere.
91. Drag and drop onto the Dock icon.
92. Consistent toasts for "saved", "added", "sent".
93. App icon and menu bar icon redrawn in the new identity.
94. Localised interface: Tamil and Hindi menus.
95. A proper Settings window with sections instead of one long panel.

## G. Privacy and security (see PUBLIC-RELEASE.md for the full threat table)

96. **Developer ID signing, notarization and signed updates.**
97. **App Sandbox** with the minimum entitlements.
98. **"Erase everything" button**: keys, permissions, data folder.
99. Encrypt friends' messages at rest with a Keychain-held key.
100. Never capture the screen when a password field or private window is in front; a list of apps never to capture.
101. Privacy dashboard: a live list of what was sent, to which service, today.
102. Certificate pinning for the AI and speech hosts.
103. App lock with Touch ID after the Mac wakes.
104. SECURITY.md with a contact, and a software bill of materials per release.
105. Automatic check in the build that no new network address was added without the policy being updated.

## H. Architecture and code quality

106. Rename the `leanring-buddy` target and files to `Thambi`.
107. Split `MainWindow.swift` and `CompanionManager.swift` into one file per page and per concern.
108. Move the library to SQLite with full-text search for large libraries.
109. One `StudyAI` path for every model call, with retries, cost tracking and a provider switch (cloud, local).
110. Continuous integration on GitHub: unit tests, a Release build, the release audit, on every push.
